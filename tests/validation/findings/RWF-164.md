## RWF-164 — BL-122's loader-reach inventory counted the probe's own calls

**Status:** Fixed (BL-128)
**Failure class:** a false record (no analyzer verdict)
**Defect class:** — (measurement)
**Severity:** P2 / the record an open owner decision rests on

**Discovered:** by task BL-128, recording where Node's loaders touch each
member of BL-122's inventory (`scripts/bl128-loader-member-routes/callsites.cjs`,
a stack per call). `globalThis.String()`, which the inventory attributed
to the ES module loader on every release, had one caller: the probe's
own `String(require(…).default)`, run in `Module._compile` of
`probe-reach.cjs` before recording stopped.

**What was wrong.** `probe-reach.cjs` ran `require(path.join(fixture, …))`,
`String(…)` of the result, and `import(require("url").pathToFileURL(…).href)
.then(…)` with recording on. Eight rows of
`scripts/bl122-trust-boundary-reach/reach-releases.md` were the probe's,
on every release: `path.join` (`cjs`, `require-esm`, `import`),
`path.normalize` (`require-esm`, `import`: called by that `path.join`),
`url.pathToFileURL` (`import`) and `globalThis.String` (`require-esm`,
`import`). The distinct members are 52, not 55, and 43, not 45, without
`import()`.

**Where it was relied on.** ADR 0010, "Proposed decision — BL-122":
"What was measured" (the O4 summary), "(W-members)" (55 and 45) and
"(W-loader)" (`globalThis.String` among the globals the ES module loader
calls); `scripts/bl122-trust-boundary-reach/results.md` § 1's summary
table. No oracle case, open-soundness-defect record or verdict rests on
the removed rows: BL-122's end-to-end routes (RWF-161, RWF-162, RWF-163)
are each on a member the corrected table keeps.

**Fix (BL-128).** The probe computes its paths and URL before recording
and stringifies after it, and awaits `import()` instead of chaining
`.then` (which reads `Promise[Symbol.species]`). `reach-releases.md` is
regenerated on the same thirteen releases: exactly those eight rows are
gone and no other row changed. A correction is appended to `results.md`
and noted in ADR 0010 ("Note — task BL-128"); the proposed section is not
edited.
